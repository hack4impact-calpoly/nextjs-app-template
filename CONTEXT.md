# Coffee Shop

The shop presents its offerings to customers and lets the team maintain the menu.

## Language

**Offering**:
A drink or food item available from the shop, with a name, description, price, and category.
_Avoid_: Product, menu record

**Special offering**:
An offering the shop currently features on its Specials page. Being featured does not necessarily imply a reduced price.
_Avoid_: Discount, sale item

**Menu**:
The collection of offerings customers can browse.

**Offering management**:
The team's activity of maintaining offerings shown to customers.
_Avoid_: Customer ordering
